IOC checker

Paste IPs, domains, URLs, file hashes, JA3/JA4 fingerprints, emails or CVEs, in any mix and even defanged. Indicators are sanitized, de-duplicated and typed, then you pick the platforms and search them all in one click. No API keys needed.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere until you open a search. Opening a platform means that platform sees the indicator, so don't search confidential employer data on third-party sites. JA3/JA4 lookups are limited on public platforms; some links are search entry points, and a few platforms need an account.

Investigation pivots

IP → reputation (AbuseIPDB, GreyNoise), exposure (Shodan/Censys), first-seen and passive DNS (VT, OTX). Domain → registration age (Whois), certificates (crt.sh), scans (urlscan). Hash → VT detections, sandbox reports, MalwareBazaar family tags.

Confidence habits

Reputation ≠ verdict. Check first-seen dates, shared hosting/CDN, and whether the IOC appears in your own telemetry before escalating. Record source and date for every indicator.

Handling

Defang before sharing. Never open live URLs from a work machine; use a sandbox. Follow your organisation's policy on submitting indicators to public services.
